# Support

**Walkie**

Email [nckoffeeking@gmail.com](mailto:nckoffeeking@gmail.com) for help, privacy questions, and account deletion.

## How does push-to-talk work?

Walkie sends a voice clip. It is not a live call. You pick a person or a group, then talk. The on-screen button starts when you press and stops when you let go. On an iPhone with an Action Button, a shortcut starts on one press and stops on the next. It is not press and hold, because the phone does not report when you let go. The same shortcut can be added in Control Center.

The clip is encrypted on your phone and uploaded. Apple wakes the other phones. Those phones download the clip and play it. Expect a short delay. While a clip is playing, that phone does not start a new one.

## Why does Walkie need the microphone?

The microphone records the voice clip while you are talking. Walkie uses it only then, for the people you choose. It does not listen when you are not talking.

## Who can message me?

At launch, someone you are not connected with can send a request. Nothing comes through until you accept. A block in either direction stops matching, stops new conversations, and stops adding that person to a group. A group can hold up to 20 people, including the person who creates it.

## How long are voice clips kept?

At launch, a voice clip is deleted from the server 3 days after sending. The message stays and shows "Voice clip expired". Text stays so people who were in the conversation when it was sent can still read it.

## How do I block someone?

You can block someone. A block in either direction stops matching, new conversations, and adding each other to a group. This version does not include a report button.

## How do friends find me?

By email, not by phone number. If you signed in with an email code, that address can be used to find you, and you can turn that off. If you used Sign in with Apple, the private relay address is hidden. Add an email your friends have, then enter the code Walkie sends to that inbox.

## How do I delete my account?

Email [nckoffeeking@gmail.com](mailto:nckoffeeking@gmail.com) and ask for the Walkie account to be deleted. The steps are below. Signing out does not delete the account.

## Delete your account

Email [nckoffeeking@gmail.com](mailto:nckoffeeking@gmail.com) from an address you can open.

1. Use the subject line **Delete my Walkie account**.
2. Include your display name in Walkie.
3. Say whether you signed in with Apple or with an email code.
4. Include that email address if you know it. A private relay address from Sign in with Apple counts.
5. Write that you want the Walkie account deleted.

Signing out is not the same as deleting the account. Sign out is in settings. It clears the session on that phone, clears the push tokens, and leaves the push-to-talk channel. It does not delete the account.

When the account is deleted, the login, the profile, the discoverable email record, the device rows, and the push tokens are removed. Your memberships are marked as left. Encrypted messages you already sent stay for the people still in those conversations, because their phones already have them. Deletion does not remove a copy that is already on someone else's phone.
