# Delete your Walkie account

You can ask for your Walkie account to be deleted by email. You do not need the app installed to send the request.

Email [nckoffeeking@gmail.com](mailto:nckoffeeking@gmail.com) from an address you can open.

1. Use the subject line **Delete my Walkie account**.
2. Include your display name in Walkie.
3. Say whether you signed in with Apple or with an email code.
4. Include that email address if you know it. A private relay address from Sign in with Apple counts.
5. Write that you want the Walkie account deleted.

Signing out is not the same as deleting the account. Sign out is in settings. It clears the session on that phone, clears the push tokens, and leaves the push-to-talk channel. It does not delete the account.

When the account is deleted:

- The login, the profile, the discoverable email record, the device rows, and the push tokens are removed.
- Your memberships are marked as left.
- Encrypted messages you already sent stay for the people still in those conversations. Their phones already have those messages.
- Deletion does not remove a copy that is already on someone else's phone.

The same rules are in the [privacy policy]({{ '/walkie/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
