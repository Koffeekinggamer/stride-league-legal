# Delete your Stride League account

**Stride League**, operated by **Judson Miller**

You can ask for your Stride League account and the personal data that goes with it to be deleted here. You do not need the app installed, and you do not need to download it again.

This page is the web deletion link for the app: https://koffeekinggamer.github.io/stride-league-legal/delete-account/

## Delete in the app

1. Open **Stride League**.
2. Go to the **You** tab.
3. Choose **Delete account**.
4. Confirm.

Deletion of the live account starts when you confirm. That is not a freeze. You will not be able to sign in to that account again.

## Delete by email

If you cannot open the app, email **nckoffeeking@gmail.com** from the email you can check.

Use the subject line **Delete my Stride League account** and include:

- Your display name in the app
- Whether you signed in with Apple or with Google
- The email address on that Apple or Google account, if you know it
- A sentence that you want the Stride League account deleted

Do not send medical records, a photo of an ID, or your step history. Judson Miller matches the request to the account from the display name and the sign-in method.

**Response time.** A request that can be matched to an account is completed within **30 days**. You will get an email when it is done, or if the account cannot be matched and he needs one more detail.

## What is deleted

When the deletion is completed on the live database:

- Sign-in is removed. You cannot sign back in.
- Your email, if one was stored, is deleted.
- Your display name is cleared.
- Your phone timezone and push token are deleted.
- Your **personal** day-by-day step totals (the ones only you could see) are deleted.
- You are removed from races that are still going, including a race in its 12-hour catch-up window after the end.

Provider backups of that deleted data are not used to put your name or personal step history back. Those backup copies are removed within **30 days** after the live deletion.

Crash reports, if any, do not include your step totals. The crash tool keeps its own diagnostic logs for up to **90 days**, then deletes them. They are not used to rebuild your account.

## What is kept, and for how long

On a race that has **already locked**, the step totals already counted for you **stay**, so the result does not change. Your name is removed. Other people in that race see **Former member**, not your display name.

Those totals are kept for as long as that race result is kept while Stride League is operating. They are not used for advertising, not sold, and not used for anything except that locked result. They are deleted if the app is shut down.

A blank profile row may remain so the locked race still has a member to point at. The name on it is cleared. That is not an active account.

Leaving a race, or being removed by the person who created it, is not the same as deleting your account. On a locked race, leave or removal may still show the name you used.
